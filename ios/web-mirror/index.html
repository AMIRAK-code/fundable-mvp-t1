Everything here runs in your browser — no photo, measurement or profile leaves this page, matching the app's on-device promise. MANE is a grooming companion, not a medical device: hairline and density figures are heuristics, sensitive to lighting and angle, and useful for their trend across consistent scans rather than as absolute readings. For medical concerns about hair loss, see a dermatologist.
